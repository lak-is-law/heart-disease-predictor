# Running the clinic rehearsal

Use the existing local portal server and its development-only account store. Each installation supports one invited facility. Use a separate database and credentials for each rehearsal installation. Enter fictional information only.

1. Sign up for individual patient, staff and manager accounts. Choose a workspace on the sign-in page or visit `/pilot`. Existing accounts keep their private research cases.
2. Open **My account code** on the manager’s device. After verifying the facility and manager directly, the platform administrator opens the facility workspace, enters that code, and registers the clinic name, expected review window and urgent contact instructions. Verify codes directly with the account holder before granting access or issuing a visit invitation.
3. The manager adds the staff account. Staff issue a visit invitation using the patient’s account code. The patient sees the receiving facility and visit reference, saves a draft, previews it and chooses whether to share. A patient can prepare and print a draft without an invitation.
4. Staff open the inbox and confirm they read the version shown. Refresh to see later updates. Rehearse a correction, duplicate submission, withdrawal, delayed review, staff removal, shared-device sign-out and failed connection. Confirm that private drafts and withdrawn summaries stay out of the inbox.
5. For initial usability testing, ask five intended users to complete the fictional workflow on their own phones. Record completion time, help needed, and whether each can identify the receiving facility and review status. Aim for four unassisted completions within five minutes and five correct recipient/status answers.

## Decision before enrollment

The clinic names an inbox owner and backup, rehearses delayed review and signs off on appointment matching, supported languages and patient wording. The clinic and qualified Indian counsel review the actual vendors, hosting, notices, retention, access, withdrawal, incident ownership and launch-date requirements. Record decisions and unresolved issues outside the patient workflow. Disable enrollment on any wrong-recipient disclosure.

Before expanding, compare consecutive pilot visits with a baseline from usual intake. Count patient effort, total staff minutes including copying and follow-up, transcription errors, and reviews within the promised window. Record whether the clinic chooses to continue with its own results. Historical model scores are available only in the educational workstation.
