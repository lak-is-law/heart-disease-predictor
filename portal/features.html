Features

What each part of CardioSense does, in plain terms, and what it cannot do.

Core

A risk estimate from 13 inputs

Enter age, sex, chest-pain type, resting blood pressure, cholesterol, fasting glucose, resting ECG, maximum heart rate, exercise angina, ST depression, ST slope, the number of vessels seen on fluoroscopy and the thallium result.

  • The output is a probability that angiography would show coronary disease.
  • Standard mode flags at 0.50. High-sensitivity mode flags at 0.28, which caught about 88% of cases in cross-validation and also flags many people without disease.
  • Vessel count and thallium are required. If they are missing, no estimate is shown.

How it works

  1. Enter valuesType them in, pick a sample case, or import a report.
  2. ScoreThe model computes a probability in your browser.
  3. ReviewSee the estimate, the threshold used and the visuals.
Visuals

An interactive heart and ECG trace

A 3D heart with four chambers turns and opens along the four-chamber plane. The ECG trace and coronary view respond to your inputs.

  • These are teaching aids driven by the inputs. They are not measurements or images of the patient.
Import

Lab report import

Drop in a lab report and the matching fields are filled for you. CSV, JSON, HL7 and plain-text consultation notes are supported. Check every imported value before relying on it.

Supported files

CSV, JSON, HL7 v2 messages and text notes. Sample files are included in the workstation.

Try a sample
Account

Accounts and a dashboard

Create an account with your email, or sign in with Google, Microsoft, X or ChatGPT where enabled. Save assessments and review them later.

  • Saved fields are encrypted on the server with AES-256-GCM.
  • Signing out ends the session on the server, not only in your browser.
  • Delete any saved assessment, or your whole account, from the dashboard.
Desktop

macOS and Windows apps

Installers for Apple silicon and Intel Macs, and for Windows on x64 and ARM64. Every installer lists a SHA-256 checksum, and the download page can check a file for you.

Which one do I need?

On a Mac, open the Apple menu, choose About This Mac and look at Chip. On Windows, open Settings, System, About and look at System type.

Honest limits

What it cannot do

  • It was trained on 303 patients from one clinic in the 1980s, all referred for angiography. 97 were women.
  • The label is angiographic disease, not future heart attacks.
  • AUC is 0.91 (95% CI 0.87 to 0.94) with all 13 inputs and 0.84 (0.79 to 0.88) without the invasive and stress-test inputs.
  • It has not been tested prospectively or at another site. A good AUC does not show that using it improves outcomes.
  • A low estimate does not rule out disease.

Not a medical device

No regulatory clearance or exemption is claimed. Do not use it for care decisions or with real patient information.
