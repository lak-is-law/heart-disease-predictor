Features

What each part does, in plain terms, and what it cannot do.

The estimate

Enter age, sex, chest-pain type, resting blood pressure, cholesterol, fasting glucose, resting ECG, maximum heart rate, exercise angina, ST depression, ST slope, the number of vessels seen on fluoroscopy and the thallium result.

  • The output is the probability that angiography would show coronary disease.
  • Standard mode flags at 0.50. High-sensitivity mode flags at 0.28, which caught about 88% of cases in cross-validation and also flags many people without disease.
  • Vessel count and thallium are required. Without them no estimate is shown.

The heart and ECG

A 3D heart with four chambers turns and opens along the four-chamber plane. The ECG trace and coronary view respond to your inputs.

These are teaching aids driven by the inputs. They are not measurements or images of the patient.

Lab report import

Drop in a lab report and the matching fields are filled. CSV, JSON, HL7 v2 and plain-text consultation notes are supported. Sample files are included in the workstation. Check every imported value before relying on it.

Accounts and dashboard

Create an account with your email, or sign in with Google, Microsoft, X or ChatGPT where enabled. Save assessments and review them later.

  • Saved fields are encrypted on the server with AES-256-GCM.
  • Signing out ends the session on the server, not only in your browser.
  • Delete any saved assessment, or all of them, from the dashboard.

Desktop apps

Installers for Apple silicon and Intel Macs, and for Windows on x64 and ARM64. Each lists a SHA-256 checksum, and the download page can check a file for you.

On a Mac, choose Apple menu, About This Mac and read the Chip line. On Windows, open Settings, System, About and read System type.

Limits

  • Small, old data. 303 patients from one clinic in the 1980s, all referred for angiography. 97 were women.
  • The label is angiographic disease, not future heart attacks.
  • Accuracy. AUC is 0.91 (95% CI 0.87 to 0.94) with all 13 inputs and 0.84 (0.79 to 0.88) without the invasive and stress-test inputs.
  • Not validated. It has not been tested prospectively or at another site. A good AUC does not show that using it improves outcomes.
  • A low estimate does not rule out disease.
  • Not a medical device. No regulatory clearance or exemption is claimed.
