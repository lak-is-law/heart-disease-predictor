About Lakshya UK

Why we built CardioSense

Lakshya UK develops CardioSense, a research workstation for studying coronary disease. We built it so clinicians, researchers and students can follow a risk estimate back to the values that shaped it.

Explore the research

Inside a case review

A single probability leaves an important question open. Which findings contributed most? In the workstation, you can enter a sample case, inspect the contribution of each input, then change a value and compare the estimates.

Open the workstation

What the evidence shows

We evaluated the model on historical records from 303 patients referred for coronary angiography in Cleveland. With all 13 inputs, it achieved a cross-validated AUC of 0.91 (95% confidence interval 0.87–0.94).

That study measured how well the model distinguished cases within this cohort. Its effect on clinical decisions and patient outcomes has yet to be tested.
