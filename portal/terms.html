Not medical advice

CardioSense is an educational research prototype. It is not a medical device, has not been reviewed or cleared by any regulator, and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. A low estimate does not mean you are free of heart disease, and a high estimate does not mean you have it. Do not use it to make decisions about care.

If you or someone else has chest pain, trouble breathing or fainting, call your local emergency number now (112 in India) or go to the nearest hospital.

What the estimate means

The model was trained on 303 patients from one clinic in the 1980s, all referred for coronary angiography, and 46% of them had disease. The percentage it shows answers "among patients like these", and it will be far too high for people in the general population. It has not been tested at another hospital or prospectively.

Do not enter real patient information

Use synthetic or de-identified data only. You are responsible for what you enter.
