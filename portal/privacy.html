Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026. CardioSense is a research prototype run by an individual developer. This page describes what the site actually stores.

What we collect

  • Account: your email, your name (optional) and a salted scrypt hash of your password. If you sign in with Google, Microsoft, X or ChatGPT we receive the identifier, name and (where given) email from that provider. We never see your provider password.
  • Saved assessments: the 13 model inputs, the risk estimate and a short reference you type. They are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they reach the database. Do not enter names, ID numbers or real patient data.
  • Session: one essential, HttpOnly cookie that keeps you signed in. We use no advertising or analytics cookies and no third-party trackers.
  • Security log: sign-in and account events with the time and a short one-way hash of your network address, used to detect abuse. Rate limiting uses a network address for a short time.
  • Reviews: your rating and text, shown with your first name and last initial.
  • Bug reports: the text you write, the page, your browser string and optionally your email.
  • Newsletter: your email address and the time you subscribed. Anyone can type an address into the form, so no emails are sent until a confirmation step exists.

Where it is stored

The site runs on Vercel and keeps data in an Upstash Redis database. The workstation page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your network address when that page opens. Calculator inputs you do not save stay in your browser.

How we use it

To run your account, show your dashboard, prevent abuse, answer bug reports and, if you subscribed, send product updates. We do not sell data and we do not use it to train models.

Your choices

  • Delete any saved assessment, or all of them, from your dashboard.
  • Unsubscribe with the link in any newsletter, or at /unsubscribe.
  • To access, correct or delete your account or other data, use Report a bug and choose "Something else", and say what you need.

Children

CardioSense is not intended for people under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes. This page is a plain description of current practice and is not legal advice; it should be reviewed by a lawyer before the site is used beyond a prototype, in particular for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and, for EU or UK visitors, GDPR.
