Medical Device Legal, Regulatory & Compliance Disclosures

Authentic regulatory documentation governing the deployment, clinical validation, and data privacy of CardioSense AI.

FDA 21 CFR § 880.6310 Non-Device CDS

CardioSense AI meets the four criteria under Section 520(o)(1)(E) of the FD&C Act for Non-Device Clinical Decision Support (CDS) software. It is not intended to acquire, process, or analyze medical images or signals directly from diagnostic hardware; it provides transparent recommendations based on independently verifiable clinical literature (Cleveland Heart Disease validation cohort).

HIPAA 45 CFR § 164.514 Safe Harbor

The workstation operates strictly on a Zero-Cloud-Egress architecture. All biomarker transformations, logistic regressions, and 3D WebGL visualizations execute within ephemeral browser process memory or local desktop sandboxes. No protected health information (PHI) or personal identifiers are collected, transmitted, or persisted.

GDPR Article 9 Special Category Health Data

In accordance with GDPR Regulation (EU) 2016/679 Article 9(2)(h) and (j), biometric data processed during risk evaluation is strictly decoupled from patient identity. The platform does not deploy third-party trackers, telemetric cookies, or external behavioral advertising scripts.
