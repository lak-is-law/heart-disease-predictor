Clinical decision support, for study

A risk estimate you can inspect.

Enter the values from a cardiac work-up. CardioSense returns the probability that angiography would show coronary disease, and shows which values moved it.

13clinical inputs, from history, ECG, stress test and imaging
0.91cross-validated AUC with all inputs (95% CI 0.87 to 0.94)
0.84AUC without the invasive and stress-test inputs
303patients in the UCI training data, one clinic

What is inside.

One workstation for entering a case, seeing the estimate and keeping a record.

Risk estimate

Logistic regression on 13 inputs. Standard threshold 0.50, or a high-sensitivity threshold of 0.28.

3D heart

Four chambers that turn and open along the four-chamber plane. A teaching aid, not an image of the patient.

Live ECG trace

Rate and ST changes follow the inputs, so you can see how a value shifts the picture.

Lab report import

Fill the form from a CSV, JSON, HL7 v2 or consultation-note file. Check every value after import.

Encrypted records

Saved assessments are encrypted on the server (AES-256-GCM) and tied to your account. Sign-out ends the session server-side.

Desktop and browser

Use it in a browser, or install it on macOS or Windows. Each installer lists its SHA-256 checksum.

How an assessment works.

Four steps from a patient record to a saved result.

  1. Enter the case

    Type the 13 values, or import them from a lab report.

  2. Read the estimate

    See the probability, the threshold used and the flag.

  3. Inspect the drivers

    The heart and ECG respond as you change a value.

  4. Save it

    Keep the assessment on your dashboard, or delete it.

The 13 inputs

  • Age
  • Sex
  • Chest-pain type
  • Resting blood pressure
  • Cholesterol
  • Fasting glucose
  • Resting ECG
  • Max heart rate
  • Exercise angina
  • ST depression
  • ST slope
  • Vessels on fluoroscopy (required)
  • Thallium result (required)

Honest about accuracy.

Questions.

Is it a medical device?

No. It is a research prototype, not reviewed by any regulator. Do not use it to diagnose, to rule out disease or to decide care.

What if I have chest pain?

Call your local emergency number now (112 in India) or go to the nearest hospital. This site cannot help in an emergency.

How accurate is it?

On repeated cross-validation of the 303-patient training set, the area under the ROC curve is 0.91 with all 13 inputs and 0.84 without the invasive and stress-test inputs. It has not been tested at another site or prospectively. Details and limits.

What happens to the data I enter?

The calculator runs in your browser. If you save an assessment, the fields are encrypted on the server and tied to your account. Use synthetic or de-identified data only.

Which platforms are supported?

macOS on Apple silicon and Intel, Windows on x64 and ARM64, and any modern browser.
