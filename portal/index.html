How reliable is this estimate?

Measured on the 303-patient Cleveland data with 20 repeats of 5-fold cross-validation (every patient scored by a model that never saw them):

  • All 13 inputs: AUC 0.91 (95% interval 0.87 to 0.94). At the 0.28 threshold it caught 88% of cases, flagged 27% of people without disease, and missed 16 of 139 cases.
  • Using only what a first visit provides (age, sex, chest pain type, blood pressure, cholesterol, glucose, resting ECG): AUC 0.84 (0.79 to 0.88).

The data is one 1980s US clinic of patients referred for angiography. It has not been tested on other hospitals, countries or today's patients, so treat the output as a research prototype, not a diagnosis. The FFR, door-to-balloon and savings figures are illustrative models, not measurements.
