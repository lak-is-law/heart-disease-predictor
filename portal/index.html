What CardioSense does

Enter the measurements from a cardiac work-up. CardioSense returns a probability, shows which values pushed it up or down, and keeps a record of your assessments.

Estimate

A logistic-regression model turns 13 clinical inputs into the probability that angiography would show coronary disease. Choose a standard or high-sensitivity threshold.

Questions

Is CardioSense a medical device?

No. It is a research prototype and has not been reviewed by any regulator. It must not be used to diagnose, to rule out disease or to decide care.

How accurate is it?

On repeated cross-validation of the 303-patient training set, the area under the ROC curve is 0.91 using all 13 inputs, and 0.84 without the invasive and stress-test inputs. It has not been tested at any other site or prospectively. Details and limits.

What happens to the data I enter?

The calculator runs in your browser. If you choose to save an assessment, the fields are encrypted on the server and tied to your account. Use synthetic or de-identified data only.

Which platforms are supported?

macOS on Apple silicon and Intel, and Windows on x64 and ARM64. The workstation also runs in any modern browser.
